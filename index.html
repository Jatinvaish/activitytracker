Before you start

Activity Tracker records your work time on this computer for your company. Here is exactly what it collects:

  • When you are active, idle or awayFrom keyboard and mouse activity, and when the screen is locked. What you type is never recorded.
  • Activity levelHow many seconds in each 10 minutes had keyboard or mouse input.
  • Apps and websitesThe name of the app in front and, for browsers, the site name. Your admin can turn this off.
  • CallsWhether your microphone or camera is in use, so calls count as work instead of idle time.
  • ScreenshotsOnly if your admin turns them on. Taken at random times while you are active. You can delete each one within 5 minutes, before it uploads. Deleted after 30 days.

Your admin can see this data. You can see your own day, history and requests in this window at any time.
